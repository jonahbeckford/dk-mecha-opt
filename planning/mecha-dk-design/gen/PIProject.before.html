Set up your project

You lead the project. The mecha proposes hypotheses, designs experiments, and analyses the results; you decide what it may change, what counts as a better result, and which experiments go ahead. It works the same for software and for a study in the lab or the field.
Where the mecha keeps this project: its hypotheses, results and every saved cycle. Choose an empty folder, or a new one. It lives on the computer that runs the project.
On the web or a phone, Choose folder browses the paired desktop. Secrets and keys are never written to this folder. To continue the project on another computer, copy or sync the folder, or keep it in a git repository.
1. Scope
Code and configuration, or conditions, materials, protocol steps and parameters.
The outcomes, and the cases they are measured on: tests and inputs, or samples, subjects and sites.
The written specification, protocol or pre-registration. When a change is adopted, you review it against this. None yet? The mecha can draft one for your review.
What no one, person or program, may touch or be asked to do: credentials, hazards, ethics and approval limits, budget ceilings. Experiments sent to a person show these as "Stop if" and "Never".
2. What counts as a better result
Your outcome measures, each something an experiment can measure. You weigh them by answering a few comparisons, not by typing weights.
